Terms of service

Community standards and platform rules.

Sakinah exists to create a respectful and serious environment for Muslim matchmaking. By using the platform, you agree to participate with integrity, honesty, and respect for other members and the community standards.

Member responsibilities

  • Provide accurate and respectful information in your profile.
  • Use the platform for genuine, serious, and respectful communication.
  • Respect boundaries, privacy, and the dignity of other members.
  • Avoid deceptive, abusive, or exploitative behavior.

Web and Telegram accounts

Web sign-in uses Telegram verification and connects to the same profile record used by the Sakinah bot. Creating or updating a profile on the website does not create a separate account. New profiles and rejected resubmissions remain subject to review before they are shown to other members.

Moderation and safety

We reserve the right to review, restrict, or remove content or accounts that violate community standards, abuse the system, or compromise the integrity of the platform.

Premium and support

Premium features may be offered as an optional support mechanism. Access and availability may change based on product needs, moderation priorities, and service operations.
