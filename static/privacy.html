Privacy Policy

🔒
Our Commitment to Privacy

YourMeet is built on Telegram and takes your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control it.

🛡️ We never sell your personal data to third parties. Your information is used solely to operate and improve YourMeet.

📊
Data We Collect
Data Type What We Collect Purpose
Identity Name, age, gender, city Profile creation & matching
Telegram Telegram user ID, language Account identification
Photos Profile photo (Telegram file_id) Profile display & moderation
Location City name, approximate coordinates Distance-based matching
Activity Likes, matches, swipes Matching algorithm
Messages Chat messages (Premium users) Message history feature
Social Optional social handle Shared with matches only
⚙️
How We Use Your Data
  • To create and display your profile to potential matches
  • To power the matching and swiping algorithm
  • To send notifications about matches and messages via Telegram
  • To process Premium subscription payments
  • To moderate content and enforce our Terms of Service
  • To improve the App's features and performance
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
🤝
Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share limited data only in these cases:

  • With other users: Your profile (name, age, city, photo, bio) is shown to potential matches
  • Social handle: Shared only after a mutual match, never before
  • Telegram: Messages are routed through Telegram's infrastructure
  • Legal requirements: If required by law or to protect safety
🗄️
Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in a secure cloud database. Photos are stored as Telegram file references — we do not store raw image files on our servers.

  • Database is encrypted at rest
  • All API communications use HTTPS/TLS
  • Access to user data is restricted to authorized systems only
  • We regularly review and update our security practices

🗑️ When you delete your account, all your data including profile, photos, matches, and messages is permanently removed within 30 days.

🍪
Cookies & Tracking

YourMeet operates primarily through Telegram and does not use traditional browser cookies for tracking. If you access any web features of the App, we may use session tokens stored in cookies solely for authentication purposes.

We do not use third-party advertising trackers or analytics services that collect personal data.

🔞
Children's Privacy

YourMeet is strictly for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we discover a user is underage, their account will be immediately terminated and all data deleted.

If you believe a minor is using the App, please report it immediately through the App.

📋
Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the App. Continued use of YourMeet after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

💬
Contact Us

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us through the YourMeet bot on Telegram. We aim to respond to all privacy requests within 72 hours.
