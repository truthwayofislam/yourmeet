Privacy policy

How we handle personal data.

Sakinah is committed to protecting the privacy and dignity of its members. We collect only the information needed to operate a respectful matchmaking community and to support clear, safe, and intentional interactions.

Information we collect

  • Profile information such as name, age, preferences, and faith-related details.
  • Account and authentication details required for secure access.
  • Communication and moderation data necessary for safety, support, and quality control.
  • Usage data that helps us maintain service reliability, prevent misuse, and improve the experience.

How we use it

We use this information to create and maintain a respectful matchmaking environment, support profile matching, improve user experience, and ensure the platform remains useful, secure, and aligned with community standards.

Your rights

Members may request access to their data, ask for corrections, or request deletion where legally applicable. We aim to handle such requests responsibly and in a timely manner.
